## Hi there 👋
My name is Aidan, but I usually go as BashfulWizard or FunnyBone around the web. Kind of a hobbiest, not really making anything big. Just something to quell my current fixations.
I have basic knowledge in programming languages but I always strive to learn more every day.

My first *real* project is the Reddit NSFW Cleaner tool. Written in Python, it uses the reddit API to remove saved, upvoted, and downvoted posts tagged as NSFW from your profile. I'm not afraid to say that I used to have a reddit problem in the past and when searching for a tool to do this very thing, I found that it didn't exist. So I made one. It's really for people like me that might've had some religious awakening or genuinely desires to essentially "reset" their reddit account.

I always have differnt ideas for projects, and I'm sure I'll add more down the road, but I currently want to develop a game hub for Windows in the style of the New Xbox Experience era of the Xbox 360.

Some personal things about me: I love music, especually grunge. I also love everything 80's and 90's. I make video projects every now and then, including making stuff in the style of content from the 1980's and 1990's. Those are usually uploaded on my YouTube.
